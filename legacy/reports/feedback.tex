\section{Goal and Claim Hierarchy}
Establish, on a fixed memory budget, that recurrent depth buys task utility, with expressivity competitive with deeper untied dense models. The claims are strictly ordered:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
  \item \textbf{P1 (primary):} $G_T(K_{\rm lo},K_{\rm hi})>0$ at fixed memory, on data that requires depth.
  \item \textbf{P2 (memory):} reversibility makes activation memory constant in $K$ (severable from P3; \emph{not yet measured} --- the memory-saving reversible backward path is not implemented in the Tier-1 harness, so this remains a target, not a proven result).
  \item \textbf{P3 (secondary, conditional on P1):} an MoE basis recovers tying's lost depth-diversity.
\end{itemize}
The design serves P1; P2 is verified independently; P3 is staged and gated only after P1 holds.

\section{Models (the necessary set)}

\paragraph{Core $M_0$.} Tied, token-injected, additive-coupling reversible recurrence with standard MHA/GQA $+$ SwiGLU and a halting readout. State $s_k=(a_k,b_k)$:
\begin{equation}
a_{k+1}=a_k+F_\theta\!\big(\RMSNorm(b_k+x_0)\big),\quad
b_{k+1}=b_k+G_\theta\!\big(\RMSNorm(a_{k+1}+x_0)\big),
\end{equation}
exact floor-free inverse $b_k=b_{k+1}-G_\theta(\cdot),\ a_k=a_{k+1}-F_\theta(\cdot)$ (invertibility structural, not numerical; non-contractive). Halting readout with $g_k=\sigma(w^\top z_k)$:
\begin{equation}
o_K=\sum_{k=1}^{K}\Big(g_k\!\!\prod_{j<k}(1-g_j)\Big)z_k,
\end{equation}
so $g_{K_{\rm lo}}{=}1$ reproduces the depth-$K_{\rm lo}$ output: $\mathcal F_{K_{\rm lo}}\subseteq\mathcal F_{K_{\rm hi}}$, hence achievable risk monotonicity. Gradient: \textbf{reversible full BPTT through $K_{\rm hi}$} (truncation only as a calibrated fallback). Loss:
\begin{equation}
\mathcal L=\E_{K_{\rm hi}\sim q_{\rm deep}}[L_T(K_{\rm hi})]+\lambda_h[L_T(K_{\rm hi})-\sg(L_T(K_{\rm lo}))+m]_+,\quad\lambda_h\ \text{small},
\end{equation}
with hard $\mathrm{NaN}/\mathrm{Inf}$/finiteness guards and a light boundedness penalty $[\|z_k\|_{\rm RMS}-B_z]_+^2$.

\paragraph{Diagnostic variant $M_{\rm clk}$ (depth/clock upper bound).} $M_0$ with an explicit step embedding injected each iteration, $F_\theta(\RMSNorm(b_k+x_0+e_k))$ (sinusoidal or learned $e_k$). This is the cleanest possible depth signal and is the \emph{upper bound} on depth-conditioned computation. Diagnostic only; excluded from the final model unless its extrapolation cost proves acceptable.

\paragraph{Ablation models (for P3 and memory claims).} $M_0$ itself is the tied non-MoE control; $M_{\rm MoE}$ (plain softmax or sparse top-$r$ router, marginal-load balance only) for S$+$1; $M_{\rm static}$ (route frozen across depth); $M_{\rm nonrev}$ (stored activations) for the memory benefit.

\paragraph{Explicit exclusions from the core.} No Dirichlet--UCB router; no MLA; no mixture-of-softmaxes head; no quantization in the science path; no DEQ/implicit backward, consistency, spectral, or Lyapunov term; no UFID, distillation, or anytime term.

\section{Experimental Protocol (two tiers)}
\paragraph{Tier 1 --- detectability (synthetic, depth-hard).} Primary task: iterated $S_5$ permutation composition, sequence length $\ell$ tuning the required depth ($\mathrm{NC}^1$-complete word problem $\Rightarrow$ provable, non-shortcuttable depth demand). Alternates: parity (sanity floor), s--t graph reachability with tunable diameter. Run a \emph{vanilla looped-transformer positive control} (input-injected, activations stored) through the identical harness. \textbf{Purpose:} confirm $G_T>0$ is detectable and locate any failure (see \S5 triage) before spending compute on LM.
\paragraph{Tier 2 --- deployment relevance (small LM).} Byte/char-level \code{text8}/\code{enwik8} (\bpb-native) or TinyStories. Report \bpb{} and the Pareto comparison against a matched small dense transformer.
\paragraph{Curriculum (borrowed, deliberately).} Huginn-style recurrence-count sampling for $q_{\rm deep}$ and test-time depth scaling; per-step input injection. \textbf{Not} borrowed: truncated BPTT (reversibility affords full BPTT --- the core advantage) and large-model scale.
\paragraph{Scale and statistics.} $d_{\rm model}\in[256,512]$, single reversible block, context $\le1024$, multiple seeds. Evaluate $K\in\{4,8,16,32,64,128\}$; \emph{report the convergence knee first} and choose $(K_{\rm lo},K_{\rm hi})$ to straddle it; report paired bootstrap CIs on every headline $G_T$.

\section{Clock Diagnostic and Failure Triage}
Run $M_0$, the vanilla control, and $M_{\rm clk}$ on the Tier-1 task; read off the triage:
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{center}\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{@{}L{0.46\textwidth} L{0.46\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Observation} & \textbf{Localized cause / action} \\
\midrule
Control does \emph{not} scale on a provably depth-hard task & Harness/metric is broken (pairs not straddling knee, scoring bug, task too easy). Fix before anything else. \\
Control scales; $M_0$ scales & Core is sound. Proceed to Tier 2, then S$+$1. \\
Control scales; $M_0$ does not; $M_{\rm clk}$ \emph{does} & Bottleneck is the \emph{autonomous depth signal} (vanishing displacement). Pursue sustained-dynamics / velocity-state routing, or accept a mild clock with measured extrapolation cost. \\
Control scales; neither $M_0$ nor $M_{\rm clk}$ scales & Bottleneck is block \emph{expressivity/parameterization} (e.g., additive coupling too weak), not the signal. Repair the block, not the router. \\
$M_{\rm clk}$ raises $\mathrm{AEBR}$ and $G_T$ but extrapolates poorly beyond trained depths & Clock works as an upper bound; its cost is extrapolation. Justifies investing in an autonomous substitute. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}
The clock is a diagnostic ceiling, not a design commitment: it answers ``is depth-conditioned computation useful on this data at all?'' before any investment in autonomous routing.

Demoted from assumption to \emph{tested hypothesis}: ``routes specialize without a clock'' (A6 probe), ``dense soft routing is affordable'' (moot in core; S$+$1 sparse variant).

\section{Staging (each gated)}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=2.2em,itemsep=2pt]
  \item[S$+$0.] $M_0$ on Tier-1 then Tier-2, with the clock triage. Gate: P1 (paired CI) $+$ P2 $+$ Pareto.
  \item[S$+$1 (P3).] $M_{\rm MoE}$, plain/sparse router, marginal-load balance only; Dirichlet--UCB stays deleted. Gate: $\Delta_{\rm MoE}>0$ at matched budget; $\mathrm{AEBR}$/NMI as mechanism diagnostics. Accept that $M_0$ may already suffice.
  \item[S$+$2 (KV axis).] Terminal hidden-state cache with \emph{matched training} (terminal-context attention), vs.\ exact multi-depth and shared-cache baselines. Gate: $\alpha_{\rm kv}\approx0$, quality gap $\le\tau_{\rm cache}$.
  \item[S$+$3 (deployment).] \code{int6}, MLA/GQA, sparse inference. Gate: post-quant Pareto, quant gap reported.
  \item[Fallback.] Fixed-point pressure only if S$+$0 fails by instability/overthinking/extrapolation; weakest stabilizer first.
\end{description}
